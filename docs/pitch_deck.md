# PodFlow AI - Business Pitch Deck

## Problem

**Manual Podcast Production is Slow & Expensive**
- 1 episode = 4-8 hours (research, script, recording, editing)
- Cost per episode: $100-500 (human labor)
- Limited scalability

## Solution

**PodFlow AI - Autonomous Podcast Network**
- AI-powered end-to-end production
- 1 episode = 5-10 minutes
- Cost per episode: $0.50-2.00 (API costs)

## Market Opportunity

- Podcast market: $30B+ by 2030
- Indonesia: 50M+ podcast listeners
- Content creators need scalable solutions
